# 🏆 Key Findings

- 98.4% of customers were repeat buyers, showing strong customer loyalty and the importance of retention.
- Consumers made up the largest group of returning customers, followed by corporate and home office customers.
- 20–50% discounts drove the most sales, while 10–20% discounts offered a better balance between sales and profitability.
- 45.5% of repeat purchases happened after 180+ days, highlighting an opportunity to reengage customers sooner.
- March and December had notable sales spikes, suggesting clear seasonal trends.
- Campaign timing and marketing channels affected conversions and ROI, making optimization important.
- Engagement was strongly linked to retention, making it useful for identifying customers at risk of churning.
- Customer churn leads to lost revenue, making retention key to sustainable growth.
